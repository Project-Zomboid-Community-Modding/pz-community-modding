## Adoption System

The adoption system does not require you to give up ownership, copyright, or any rights in fact. It is a licensed use of your work.

### Why?
Modders become busy, burned out, or for whatever reason they can't find time to work on stuff. Using this system keep the mod alive and well rather than a cycle of revivals, each being abandoned after the next.

Repositories setup will be subject to review from volunteers in the community, working together to keep their favorite mods alive and supported.


### How To:

- Add the PZMC Steam Account as a Friend and as a Contributor to the mod in question.

- The original repo will be forked in the organization and the mod will be updated from there using github-actions.
